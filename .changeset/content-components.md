Content components: `Button`, `Card`, `Figure`, `Icon`, `Link`, `SkipLink` and `VisuallyHidden`, with the six Material Symbols Fossil's components use as React components (`CloseIcon` and the rest). `Link` takes `asChild` to style a router's link or a button, and `Icon` takes any SVG component of your own. Each ships with stories and axe checks; real-input tests cover keyboard focus, hover and the skip link.
